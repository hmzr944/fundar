# Back-office administratif externalisé / automatisé pour TPE, artisans, freelances et micro-entrepreneurs (France, 2026)

Method note: built from web searches only (about 17 calls). Direct page fetches failed with DNS errors on several French sites (tpeactu.fr, service-public.gouv.fr), so many figures below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of each page. Figures marked "unverified" could not be checked against a primary source.

## 1. Taille du marché : TPE, artisans, micro-entrepreneurs, et temps passé sur l'administratif

### Takeaway
The addressable base is huge but mostly tiny businesses: about 5 million microentreprises (fewer than 10 employees) in France, over 3 million auto-entrepreneur accounts (only about 55 % of them have revenue), and about 1.8 million artisan businesses. Recent surveys put admin time at about 1 to 5 hours a week for most bosses. There is no good recent time-spent barometer specific to TPE.

### Cited Findings
- INSEE (2023 data, published Dec 2025): close to 5 million microentreprises (MIC). They employ 17 % of salaried staff and produce 19 % of value added, out of about 5.2 million market non-farm, non-financial businesses (97 % are MIC). — [INSEE IP2097](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/fichier/version-html/8966966/IP2097.pdf); [INSEE](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8675639)
- The older INSEE figure of 4.3 million MIC (2.6 million FTE employees) is 2021 data. Do not mix it up with the 2023 figure. — [INSEE Références, Entreprises en France 2023](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/fichier/7678530/ENTFRA23-F2.pdf)
- INSEE counts 1.9 million micro-entrepreneurs "au sens économique" in 2023. That is lower than URSSAF's administrative count. — [INSEE](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8611642)
- URSSAF: 2.914 million auto-entrepreneurs were administratively active at end Dec 2024. — [Bpifrance Création / URSSAF](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/observatoire/bibliographie/auto-entrepreneurs-fin-decembre-2024)
- URSSAF: 3.186 million administratively active at end June 2025 (+204,000 in a year). Only 49.8 % declared positive revenue in Q2 2025. — [TPE Actu, Jan 2026](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/01/29/auto-entrepreneurs-chiffre-affaires-urssaf-2025/); [Bpifrance Création](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/entrepreneur/actualites/hausse-auto-entrepreneurs-actifs)
- URSSAF, end 2025: "more than 3 million" auto-entrepreneurs (about +130,000 in a year). 1.66 million (55 %) declared positive revenue in Q4 2025. — [TPE Actu](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/01/29/auto-entrepreneurs-chiffre-affaires-urssaf-2025/). The 3.186 million (June) and "more than 3 million" (December) figures may reflect seasonality or account cleanup. I did not resolve this.
- Artisanat: "1.8 million artisan businesses" and 4.2 million employees (21 % of private-sector jobs, according to U2P) are cited by a secondary blog that relies on Crédit Agricole Propulse. I found no official 2025 national CMA France figure. — [une-case-en-plus.com](https://une-case-en-plus.com/idees-cadeau/artisanat-francais-artistique-2025/)
- CMA Nouvelle-Aquitaine counted 155,449 artisan businesses on 1 Jan 2025 (-10.7 % in a year). The CMA attributes the drop partly to problems with the new Registre national des entreprises (RNE). — search extract from CMA regional sources, e.g. [CMAR PACA chiffres-clés 2025](https://cmar-paca.fr/galerie/1/e585bacee72103cc54e18f71096278cc.pdf)
- Artisan business creations: 278,700 in one year (2024, +11 % vs 2023), according to the ISM-MAAF barometer. — [Bpifrance Création, chiffres clés création artisanale](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/observatoire/bibliographie/chiffres-cles-creation-dentreprise-artisanale)
- Time spent on admin, CJD survey (April 2025, 536 business leaders): more than half spend 1 to 5 hours a week on admin tasks. Social and tax declarations and applications for public aid are the most time-consuming. — [CJD press release, 14 April 2025](https://www.cjd.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/14-avril-2025-CP-Simplification.pdf)
- EBP (software vendor, Jan 2026 infographic, commercial source): 2 to 4 hours a week on invoicing and chasing customers. — [EBP infographic](https://infos.ebp.com/hubfs/pdf/04-Infographie/ebp-infographie-ou-passe-votre-temps.pdf)
- "32 hours a month" on admin tasks is cited by Economie Matin without a clear source (unverified). — [Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=185133)
- Older benchmark (2016): about 14 hours a week of management/admin, a third of the boss's time. Too old to use as a current figure. — [Petite-Entreprise.net baromètre oct. 2016](https://www.petite-entreprise.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Barometre-TPE-PME-octobre-2016.pdf)
- Viavoice for Wiismile (March 2025, 304 TPE/PME leaders): only 16 % remember any past simplification measure. — [Institut Viavoice](https://www.institut-viavoice.com/wiismile-x-viavoice-observatoire-des-tpe_pme-la-simplification-administrative-avril-2025)

### Inferences
- The paying core is likely the roughly 1.66 million auto-entrepreneurs with revenue, plus employer TPE and artisans. It is not the 3 million+ administrative accounts, half of which are dormant.
- At 1 to 5 hours a week of admin, the time value is about 4 to 20 hours a month. At a 35-50 €/h assistant rate, that equals 140-1,000 €/month. A 20-100 €/month tool only needs to remove a small slice of that work.

### Gaps
- No official 2025/2026 national count from CMA France / INSEE for artisans was found (cma-france.fr not reached).
- No 2025/2026 survey found from the Fédération des auto-entrepreneurs, Indy, Shine, Qonto or OpinionWay that measures admin time specifically for micro-entrepreneurs.
- The URSSAF primary publication (Stat'ur / "Les auto-entrepreneurs fin décembre 2025") was not fetched directly.

## 2. Les tâches administratives écrites les plus pénibles et récurrentes

### Takeaway
The best-documented recurring pains are late payments and chasing invoices, plus social and tax declarations. A new regulatory trigger also exists: since 1 Sept 2026, every VAT-registered business, including micro-entrepreneurs, must be able to receive e-invoices. I found no survey that ranks "disputes with suppliers, insurers or telecom providers" or "contract terminations" for TPE.

### Cited Findings
- Coface 2025: 86 % of French companies faced late payments in the past 12 months. 55 % of TPE describe the impact on cash flow as "critical". The average payment delay is 49.7 days. Causes: 42 % client financial difficulty, 23 % organisational problems, 20 % deliberate delay. — [Coface](https://www.coface.com/fr/actualites-economie-conseils-d-experts/comportements-de-paiement-en-france-en-2025-86-des-entreprises-confrontees-a-des-retards-de-paiement-menacant-leur-tresorerie)
- Late payments affect about 50 % of B2B invoices, according to Cegid (vendor source). — [Cegid](https://www.cegid.com/fr/?p=14331)
- A Senate bill (ppl25-083) to toughen sanctions against bad payers was reportedly adopted by the Senate on 19 Feb 2026. Its final status was not verified. — [Sénat ppl25-083](https://www.senat.fr/leg/ppl25-083.pdf); [Swapn](https://www.swapn.fr/blog/retards-de-paiement-sanctions)
- CJD 2025: social and tax declarations and applications for public aid are the most time-consuming admin tasks. — [CJD](https://www.cjd.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/14-avril-2025-CP-Simplification.pdf)
- E-invoicing (vendor sources, not official): from 1 Sept 2026, all VAT-registered businesses established in France must be able to receive e-invoices, including micro-entrepreneurs under VAT franchise. Issuing and e-reporting become mandatory for TPE/PME/micro on 1 Sept 2027. — [Cegid calendar](https://www.cegid.com/fr/facture-electronique-obligatoire/calendrier-facture-electronique/); [Abby](https://abby.fr/guide/facturation-electronique/facturation-electronique-date); [Sage](https://www.sage.com/fr-fr/blog/calendrier-facture-electronique/)
- Lawyer's estimate: each formality takes about 30 to 40 extra minutes (as quoted in press, anecdotal). — [Le Journal des Entreprises, "Ces PME confrontées à la folie administrative"](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/dossier/ces-pme-confrontees-la-folie-administrative-2074313)

### Inferences
- Chasing unpaid invoices (relance, then mise en demeure, then LRAR, then injonction de payer) is the best-evidenced pain. It fits the product's multi-week follow-up and LRAR engine exactly. It is also something free ChatGPT does not do: ChatGPT can draft the letter, but it does not chase on day 15, 30 and 45 or post the LRAR.
- The 2026-2027 e-invoicing reform is creating confusion and a demand for help. It is mainly a software and PDP (plateforme agréée) topic, though, not a letter-writing one.

### Gaps
- I found no French survey that quantifies how often TPE have disputes with insurers, banks, telecom or energy providers, or how often they terminate pro contracts.
- No source measures how much admin work public-tender paperwork (DUME, attestations) represents for TPE/artisans.
- No figure found for the frequency of URSSAF attestation de vigilance requests (mandatory for contracts of 5,000 € or more; regulatory fact, but no usage statistics found).

## 3. Offres existantes et prix

### Takeaway
Human delegation costs about 20-65 €/h, or 500-2,000 €+/month as a package. Online legal and admin subscriptions sit at about 29-30 € HT/month. Neobank subscriptions for freelancers cost 9-45 € HT/month. LegalPlace (which has absorbed Legalstart) has raised 70 M€ to build an "agent IA" that handles TPE accounting and formalities: this is the main well-funded threat. In the US, Lindy-style agents cost about $30-200/month.

### Cited Findings
**Human assistants / outsourced secretarial services**
- Independent administrative assistant: about 20-35 €/h for a beginner, 35-50 €/h with experience, 45-65 €/h for a specialist (business-plan template site, not institutional). — [modelesdebusinessplan.com](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/assistant-administratif-independant-tarif)
- Independent secretaries: 20-50 € HT/h (30-40 € in the Paris region, 20-30 € elsewhere). Monthly packages run from about 500 € for 10 h/month to 2,000 €+. — [modelesdebusinessplan.com](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/secretaire-independante-tarif)
- Phone answering platforms: up to 1.50 €/call for advanced services, or a monthly package. — [Companeo](https://www.companeo.com/permanence-telephonique/guide/plateforme-telephonique-prix)
- Outsourced payroll: about 15-30 € per payslip. — [Keobiz](https://www.keobiz.fr/le-mag/prix-d-un-bulletin-de-paie/)
- Freelance office manager cost guide: [Copeps](https://copeps.fr/actualites/cout-office-manager-freelance/) (figures not extracted).

**Online legal/admin platforms**
- Assistance Legalstart: 29.90 € HT/month (35.88 € TTC) or 269 € HT/year, no commitment, 30-day free trial with some packs. — [Legalstart](https://www.legalstart.fr/assistance-juridique/); [LegalPlace guide "prix Legalstart" (prices observed June 2026)](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/prix-legalstart/)
- LegalPlace legal assistance subscription: 29 € HT/month. A recent review warns about tacit renewal. — [Tool-advisor](https://tool-advisor.fr/creation-entreprise/comparatif/legalplace/); [lancetavie.fr](https://lancetavie.fr/legalplace-avis/)
- LegalPlace raised 70 M€ (XAnge, Eurazeo, Move Capital) and bought Legalstart for cash (announced 5-6 May 2026). The combined group has about 500 staff and about 20 % of French company creations. It targets more than 100 M€ revenue in 2026 after 60 % growth. It plans an AI agent that manages "both the accounts and the regulatory paperwork" of TPE. The two brands stay separate, but tech and roadmaps converge. — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/05/06/legalplace-officialise-le-rachat-de-son-concurrent-legalstart-apres-une-levee-de-70-millions-deuros/); [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/mag/378-intelligence-artificielle/104192-legalplace-leve-70-millions-deuros-et-acquiert-legalstart.html); [XAnge](https://www.xange.vc/press/legalplace-70m); [info.fr](https://info.fr/legalplace-rachete-legalstart-70-millions-ia-entrepreneurs/)
- Legal constraint: article 54 of the 1971 law reserves legal consultation for regulated professions, with criminal penalties. This limits what an AI agent may present as legal advice. — [info.fr](https://info.fr/legalplace-rachete-legalstart-70-millions-ia-entrepreneurs/)

**Neobanks / admin tools**
- Qonto plans for freelancers and micro-businesses (grid dated April 2026): Basic, Smart and Premium at 9 / 19 / 39 € HT per month billed annually, or 11 / 23 / 45 € HT billed monthly. — [Qonto price grid archived by MoneyVox, April 2026](https://www.moneyvox.fr/tarif-bancaire/qonto/pdf/tarifs-pro-avril-2026.pdf); [Milesopedia](https://milesopedia.fr/guides/qonto-tarifs/)
- Indy, Shine and Pennylane prices were not retrieved (gap).

**French AI-agent startups for SMB admin**
- Limova (Nice): AI assistants sold as an "équipe d'assistants" for TPE leaders. No funding data found. — [limova.ai](https://www.limova.ai/)
- Swiftask raised 1.55 M€ (enterprise AI agents, not specific to TPE). — [Business Times](https://business-times.fr/actualites/agents-ia-entreprise-swiftask-levee-fonds/)
- Voice Pilot (Lyon): self-serve AI voice agents for artisans, small law firms and independents. — [voice-pilot.ai](https://voice-pilot.ai/blog/panorama-agents-vocaux-ia-francais-2026)
- Graneet (ERP for construction) raised 7 M€ (Sept 2026, according to a search extract). — [Licorne Society levées](https://www.licornesociety.com/levees-de-fonds)
- "Kompa", a startup doing admin for artisans that reportedly raised 1.5 M€ (founded 2025), appeared only in a search-engine summary. A dedicated search found no corroboration, so it is unverified. — [independant.io top levées 2026](https://independant.io/top-levees-fonds-france-2026/)
- French Tech weekly: AI captured 82 % of capital raised in 14-18 Sept 2026. — [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/les-levees-de-fonds-de-la-french-tech-du-14-au-18-septembre-2026-422-millions-deuros-lia-capte-82-des-capitaux/463537)

**US analogue**
- Lindy (AI agents/assistant): Plus $49.99, Pro $99.99, Max $199.99 per month (several sources). One source checked on 19 Sept 2026 gives Plus at $29.99/user. Usage is credit-based, with a 7-day trial and no free tier. — [CostBench](https://www.costbench.com/software/ai-automation/lindy/); [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/lindy-ai-pricing-explained); [Automation Atlas](https://automationatlas.io/answers/lindy-pricing-explained-2026/)

### Inferences
- Price anchors a TPE already sees: about 30 €/month for "assistance juridique" (Legalstart/LegalPlace) and 9-45 €/month for a bank account. A human assistant costs 35-50 €/h. A per-task price of 15-50 € or a 29-79 €/month subscription would sit between "software" and "one hour of human help".
- LegalPlace/Legalstart are the closest well-funded competitors moving toward agentic admin. Their core business is formalities and creation, not disputes or chasing payments.

### Gaps
- No primary Malt rate data was found for assistants administratifs (Malt pages were not reached).
- Prices for Officéo, Ubiq or virtual-assistant networks were not retrieved.
- Admin pricing for Indy, Shine, Pennylane and Dougs was not retrieved.
- Pilot.com (US bookkeeping) pricing was not retrieved.

## 4. Usage de l'IA générative par les TPE/PME en France et ses limites

### Takeaway
GenAI use by TPE/PME leaders has jumped: about 26 % of businesses use AI (France Num 2025), about 40 % in the 2026 edition, and Bpifrance reports 55 % of leaders using GenAI at end 2025. Regular use is still low (17 %), though, and the main use is producing written content. This supports the founder's thesis that drafting is commoditised and execution is not.

### Cited Findings
- France Num barometer 2025 (DGE, 11,021 businesses, of which 7,878 TPE, surveyed 26 March to 18 April 2025): 26 % use AI, versus 13 % in 2024, mostly to produce text, voice or images. Use is 41 % in the digital sector versus 9 % in agriculture. — [entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/espace-presse/france-num-presente-la-6e-edition-de-son-barometre-annuel-sur-la-transformation); [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3237194); [Le Monde Informatique](https://www.lemondeinformatique.fr/actualites/lire-plus-de-pme-et-tpe-francaises-adoptent-la-genai-97992.html)
- France Num barometer 2026: 40 % of TPE-PME use AI (+14 points vs 2025), according to a search extract of service-public (page not fetched). Sample size is inconsistent across releases (9,000 vs 9,655). — [entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr A19079](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/actualites/A19079); [economie.gouv.fr (28/09/2026)](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3242215)
- France Num 2025: 67 % of TPE/PME have invoicing software and 65 % have accounting software. 75 % dedicated a budget to digital in 2024 (-4 points). In the 2023-24 edition, about 50 % spent 100-2,000 €/year on digital and 29 % spent more than 2,000 €. — [Solutions Numériques](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/la-quasi-totalite-des-dirigeants-constatent-les-benefices-du-numerique/); [Solutions Numériques, invoicing](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/decideur-entreprise/tpe-pme-lequipement-en-logiciels-de-facturation-en-hausse-de-13/)
- Bpifrance Le Lab (conjuncture survey of about 4,700 TPE-PME, Nov-Dec 2025, published Jan 2026): 55 % of leaders use GenAI, versus 31 % at end 2024 and 15 % at end 2023. Only 17 % use it regularly. Main uses: written content (72 %), research and data analysis (67 %), translation (34 %). These figures come from a search extract and the Bpifrance release itself was not fetched. — [Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/seuls-32-des-pme-et-eti-utilisent-reellement-lintelligence-artificielle-selon-bpifrance-2120954); [ActuIA, Bpifrance Le Lab](https://www.actuia.com/acteur/bpifrance-le-lab/)
- Bpifrance Le Lab, end 2023: 3 % of TPE-PME leaders used GenAI regularly and 12 % occasionally. — [ActuIA](https://www.actuia.com/actualite/etude-de-bpifrance-le-lab-lutilisation-des-ia-generatives-par-les-dirigeants-de-pme-tpe-est-encore-tres-limitee/)
- Bpifrance June 2025 (more than 1,200 PME/ETI leaders): about 6 in 10 see AI as a matter of survival within 3-5 years, but only 43 % have an AI strategy. Main barriers: implementation cost, risk of misuse or data exposure, and difficulty identifying use cases. — [Option Finance](https://www.optionfinance.fr/actualites/ia-une-question-de-survie-pour-les-dirigeants-de-pme-et-eti-mais-pas-encore-dadoption-generalisee.html); [ActuIA](https://www.actuia.com/actualite/lia-dans-les-pme-et-eti-francaises-un-territoire-encore-a-explorer-selon-bpifrance/)
- Ifop for Oxygen/Mastercard (May 2024, TPE leaders): "lack of usefulness" is the top barrier to AI (59 %). — cited via search extract; primary not fetched (see [findskill.ai summary](https://findskill.ai/fr/ia-pour-petite-entreprise/))
- France Num 2025: 52 % of leaders worry about data hacking. — [entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/espace-presse/france-num-presente-la-6e-edition-de-son-barometre-annuel-sur-la-transformation)
- CMA / artisanat.fr published an AI white paper for artisans in July 2025. — [artisanat.fr Livre blanc IA](https://www.artisanat.fr/system/files/medias/file/2025/07/20250701-LivreBlanc_IA.pdf)

### Inferences
- Text generation is the dominant GenAI use (72 %), which confirms that "writing a letter" is already being done for free. Regular use is still only 17 %, and "use case unclear" and "not useful" are the top barriers. A product framed as "a job done", for example "your unpaid invoice chased to LRAR", fits those barriers better than "an AI assistant".
- Data-exposure concerns (52 %) argue for French hosting and GDPR messaging.

### Gaps
- No survey found on the limits TPE experience specifically with ChatGPT-drafted admin letters (errors, no follow-up).

## 5. Consentement à payer et prix d'abonnement acceptés par les TPE

### Takeaway
I found no direct French survey of willingness to pay for admin delegation. The proxies are market prices for comparable products (29-30 € HT/month for legal assistance, 9-45 € HT/month for neobanks, $30-200/month for US AI agents) and France Num's annual digital spending bands (100-2,000 €/year for about half of businesses).

### Cited Findings
- About 50 % of TPE/PME spend 100-2,000 €/year on digital, 29 % spend more than 2,000 € (France Num 2023-24). — [Solutions Numériques](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/la-quasi-totalite-des-dirigeants-constatent-les-benefices-du-numerique/)
- Legalstart and LegalPlace assistance subscriptions cost 29.90 € and 29 € HT/month. — see Section 3 sources.
- Qonto costs 9-45 € HT/month for freelancers. — [MoneyVox grid](https://www.moneyvox.fr/tarif-bancaire/qonto/pdf/tarifs-pro-avril-2026.pdf)
- Human secretarial packages start at about 500 €/month for 10 h. — [modelesdebusinessplan.com](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/secretaire-independante-tarif)

### Inferences
- Half of TPE spend at most about 170 €/month on all digital tools combined, so a single admin-agent subscription probably needs to stay below about 30-50 €/month to fit a typical budget. Per-task fees (for example per relance sequence or per LRAR sent) may convert better among the many micro-entrepreneurs with irregular needs and low revenue.

### Gaps
- No WTP survey (Van Westendorp or similar) for admin delegation among French TPE/freelancers was found.
- No conversion or churn benchmarks for TPE SaaS were found.

## 6. Canaux d'acquisition

### Takeaway
The expert-comptable is the trusted, central advisor of TPE, so it is the strongest prescription channel. CMA/CCI networks are actively pushing AI (CMA white paper). Evidence for BNI, LinkedIn, SEO and marketplaces was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Cegid-OpinionWay (France, 2022): 91 % of leaders say they fully trust their expert-comptable, who saves them time and gives useful advice. Digital tools for exchanging with the accountant are a lever. — [Cegid Baromètre Entrepreneurs](https://www.cegid.com/fr/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2022/12/Cegid_Barometre-Entrepreneurs-Cegid-OpinionWay-FV.pdf)
- Belgium 2025 (Moore / Trends-Tendances / OECCBB): close to 1 in 2 leaders sees the accountant as the first contact for structural decisions, including digitalisation. — [OECCBB](https://blog.oeccbb.be/fr/article/que-retenir-du-barometre-tpepme-2025-trends-tendances-/28786)
- CMA network published an AI white paper for artisans (July 2025), showing that chambers are acting as AI prescribers. — [artisanat.fr](https://www.artisanat.fr/system/files/medias/file/2025/07/20250701-LivreBlanc_IA.pdf)
- LegalPlace/Legalstart captures about 20 % of company creations, so it controls a major acquisition entry point at business creation. — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/05/06/legalplace-officialise-le-rachat-de-son-concurrent-legalstart-apres-une-levee-de-70-millions-deuros/)

### Inferences
- For a solo founder, the realistic channels are partnerships with experts-comptables (white-label or referral for "relance/recouvrement amiable" letters), local CMA/CCI workshops, and SEO on specific intents such as "lettre de relance impayé", "mise en demeure facture" or "résilier contrat pro". These are inferred, not measured.

### Gaps
- No data found on BNI, LinkedIn, Malt or marketplace acquisition costs or effectiveness for TPE SaaS.
- No French 2025-2026 survey found on where TPE discover software tools.
