# B2B2C: CSE, employee-benefit platforms, mutuelles/insurers and banks as buyers of "done-for-you" dispute/administrative assistance (France, 2026)

Method note (read first): research done on 2026-10-13 with web search only. Direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy 403), so every finding below comes from search-result snippets and summaries, not from a full read of the page. Precise figures should be re-checked on the source page before being quoted externally. Many sources are vendor or marketing pages (cse-guide.fr, Edenred, Klaro, Zevra directory); they are flagged as such.

## 1. CSE: how many, how big the ASC budget is, and existing legal-assistance offers and prices

### Takeaway
The best official data is in percentages, not counts. In 2023, 35.8% of private firms with 10+ employees had an elected staff body, and those firms cover 76.7% of employees. For firms with 50+ employees, which have a mandatory ASC budget, coverage is above 80%. A vendor estimate puts the number of CSE at "a little under 100,000". The ASC budget averages about 0.8–1% of gross payroll, often cited as roughly 150–300 € per employee per year, but there is no recent official survey. Legal-assistance offers sold to CSE exist through Kalidea/Up, Comitéo/Swile (with Lefebvre Dalloz), Leeto, Ekie (also via Edenred) and Solucia PJ. None publishes a price per employee. They mostly offer information and advice from lawyers, not someone who handles the dispute end to end.

### Cited Findings
**Number of CSE / coverage**
- DARES: in 2023, 35.8% of private non-agricultural firms with 10+ employees had at least one elected staff body, covering 76.7% of employees in that scope (down 0.4 pt from 2022). — [DARES Résultats, Les instances de représentation des salariés en 2023 (PDF)](https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/0f0f8bcdceb5e442417a46304fbd1d95/Dares_R%C3%A9sultats_Les_instances_de_repr%C3%A9sentation_des_salari%C3%A9s_en_2023.pdf); [CSE Matin summary](https://www.csematin.com/fonctionnement/mandats/pratiques/representation-des-salaries-35-8-des-entreprises-couvertes-par-une-irp-en-2023-dares.html)
- By size: only 25.4% of firms with 10–49 employees have a CSE (73.6% have no elected body at all). 82.6% of firms with 50–299 employees have an elected body (CSE or CE). — same DARES 2023 source
- 2024 update: 37.2% of firms with 10+ employees have an elected body (as reported by a consultancy summarising DARES; not checked against the primary source). — [APSSS Consulting](https://apsss-consulting.fr/cse-cssct-syndicats-entreprises-2024-dares/)
- Vendor estimate: "a little under 100,000 CSE" in France, mostly in firms with 50–200 employees, covering about 20 million employees. The site sells to CSE and gives no primary source, so treat this as UNVERIFIED. — [cse-guide.fr, Marché du CSE](https://www.cse-guide.fr/marche-du-cse/)
- Since 1 Jan 2020 a CSE is mandatory from 11 employees (once the headcount has been reached for 12 consecutive months). — [Éditions Tissot](https://www.editions-tissot.fr/actualite/representants-du-personnel-ce/enquete-nationale-des-cse-2025-un-engagement-fort-mais-de-plus-en-plus-contraint)

**Budgets**
- From 50 employees the CSE has two budgets. The operating budget (budget de fonctionnement) is 0.20% of gross payroll up to 1,999 employees and 0.22% above that. The ASC budget has no legal minimum. Without an agreement, it cannot be lower, as a share of payroll, than the previous year's. Branch agreements can set a floor; for example, IDCC 1672 (insurance sector) sets 0.60% of gross payroll. Below 50 employees there is no mandatory budget. — [Legisocial](https://www.legisocial.fr/representants-du-personnel/comite-social-economique-cse/moyens/budgets-dispose-cse.html); [Tripalio, IDCC 1672 agreement (PDF)](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IDCC-1672-Accord-professionnel-financement-CSE.pdf)
- Average ASC budget is about 0.8% of gross payroll (a Senate estimate from about 2013, still repeated in 2025 guides). The DARES 2004 survey gave 0.84%. No recent national census exists. — [miroirsocial.com](https://www.miroirsocial.com/disparite-tres-importantes-des-budgets-des-activites-sociales-et-culturelles-du-cse); [cse-guide.fr Budget ASC](https://www.cse-guide.fr/le-comite-social-et-economique-cse/budget-asc/)
- Range: about 0.3% of payroll in some SMEs to more than 2% in large companies. — [cse-guide.fr / search summary](https://www.cse-guide.fr/le-comite-social-et-economique-cse/budget-asc/) (vendor, UNVERIFIED)
- A sample of 12 CSE (2024–2025): mean 0.95% of payroll, median 1%, and an average ASC budget of about 300 € per head. This is not representative. — [CC Entreprise blog](https://ca-comitentreprise.com/blog/pratique/budget-asc-1-pourcent-realiste/)
- Order of magnitude 150–250 € per employee per year for a company with 2 M€ of payroll (simulation, not a survey). — [Influence CE, Budget CSE 2026](https://influence-ce.fr/budget-cse-guide-complet-2026/)
- National ASC total of about 5 bn €/year, claimed by a specialist site with no verifiable source (UNVERIFIED). — [cse-guide.fr](https://www.cse-guide.fr/marche-du-cse/)
- Legal assistance for the CSE is generally paid from the operating budget (0.2%), according to Pluxee. Help for employees' personal problems would normally fall under ASC (inference, see below). — [Pluxee blog](https://www.pluxee.fr/blog/assistance-juridique-cse/)

**Existing legal/administrative assistance offers sold to CSE or employers**
- **Leeto**: legal service in two parts: elected members ask questions to specialised lawyers and get answers within 24 hours, plus a separate assistance service for employees' everyday problems. No public price; quote only. — [cse-guide.fr Leeto](https://www.cse-guide.fr/reseau-partenaire/leeto/); [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/tools/leeto/)
- **Comitéo (part of Swile since 2024)**: "L'appel expert" run with Lefebvre Dalloz. It covers elected members (employment and social-protection law) and employees (disputes with third parties), with written answers and availability 6 days a week. No public price. — [Comitéo, Assistance juridique CSE](https://www.comiteo.net/assistance-juridique-cse/)
- **Kalidea (Up group)**: legal service for the CSE and its beneficiaries (employment-law information, assistance, protection). One-year subscription without tacit renewal. No public price. — [up-kalidea.fr](https://up-kalidea.fr/juridique-cse-salaries/); [Officiel CSE listing](https://www.officielce.com/fournisseur/fonctionnement-du-cse/assistance-et-aide-aux-salaries/kalidea-ce-groupe-up)
- **Meyclub / ProwebCE (now Edenred Solutions CSE)**: ticketing and gift vouchers. No legal-assistance offer found. — [cse-guide.fr MeyClub](https://www.cse-guide.fr/reseau-partenaire/meyclub/); [logiciels.pro](https://www.logiciels.pro/comparateur-logiciels-de-gestion-du-ce-cse-comite-dentreprise-comite-social-et-economique-cse/)
- **Solucia PJ** (a legal-protection insurer) sells to CSE: legal information for employees and their families, Monday to Saturday, plus help in case of dispute. No price in the snippet. — [cse-guide.fr Solucia PJ](https://www.cse-guide.fr/reseau-partenaire/solucia-pj/)
- **Ekie (formerly Avostart), the "Legal Care" category**: annual subscription paid by the company, unlimited access for employees to legal, tax and administrative help from about 50 law firms. Covers advice up to amicable resolution; court proceedings are excluded. Distributed by Edenred since about 2023. Reach claims are inconsistent: 50,000 people / 100+ companies (press), 200,000+ employees (website), 500,000+ employees / 1,000+ CSE and companies (App Store). B2B price not public ("Être contacté"). Individual consumer prices: 9.90 €/month standard, 14.90 € with phone, 19.90 € with legal-protection insurance. — [ekie.co](https://www.ekie.co/); [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/professions/337-legaltech/80460-avostart-devient-ekie-service-legalcare-destination-entreprises.html); [RH Matin](https://www.rhmatin.com/qvt/bien-etre-travail/assistance-juridique-des-salaries-la-plateforme-ekie-veut-gagner-en-visibilite-avec-edenred.html); [Edenred, Ekie partenaire](https://www.edenred.fr/ekie-partenaire); [Edenred magazine](https://www.edenred.fr/magazine/notre-actualite/edenred-france/bien-etre-des-salaries-le-legal-care-nouveau-levier-qvt); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ci/app/ekie/id6768680179); [Presse Agence](https://presseagence.fr/paris-ekie-la-start-up-qui-revolutionne-le-legal-care-co-fondee-par-raphael-jabol/)
- Price points found:
  - Competing CSE legal-assistance providers charge "around 600 € HT per month", usually bundled with litigation handling. The base is unclear (per CSE, not per employee) and the date is unclear. — [Pluxee blog](https://www.pluxee.fr/blog/assistance-juridique-cse/)
  - Lamy Liaisons phone legal assistance (HR side): 325 € HT for a 5-question pack; HR subscriptions from 54 to 107 € HT per month depending on company size. — [Lamy Liaisons boutique](https://boutique.lamy-liaisons.fr/liaisons-sociales/assistance-juridique-telephonique.html)

### Inferences
- Rough addressable ASC money: with ~0.8–1% of payroll and ~150–300 €/employee/year, a per-member add-on at 0.5–2 €/employee/month (6–24 €/year) would take 2–15% of a typical ASC budget. That is plausible but competes with holiday vouchers and ticketing, which dominate ASC spending. Not sourced; derived from the figures above.
- Current CSE "legal" offers are mostly expert hotlines (Q&A answered within 24 hours). The founder's differentiator is execution: drafting letters, sending LRAR, reminders, escalation to the médiateur, follow-up. That positioning is not visible in the offers found.
- The most realistic channel into CSE is probably not 100k individual CSE but the platforms (Leeto, Swile/Comitéo, Kalidea/Up, Edenred/ProwebCE, Pluxee). They already list "legal care" partners; Ekie via Edenred is the template. This is an inference.
- Firms with 11–49 employees have a CSE but no mandatory budget, so the realistic target is CSE in firms with 50+ employees.

### Gaps
- No official absolute count of CSE (DARES gives percentages only; the count would need the full DARES PDF).
- No public price per employee per month for Leeto, Comitéo, Kalidea, Ekie or Solucia for CSE. Quotes are needed.
- No recent breakdown of what ASC money is spent on (holidays, vouchers, ticketing shares), and no recent DARES or ministry survey of ASC budgets.
- Juritravail, Wengo and Allo-juriste CSE offers were not found in the searches.

## 2. Employer employee-benefits market (avantages salariés, conciergerie, "legal care")

### Takeaway
"Legal care" exists as an employer-paid benefit category in France, led by Ekie and distributed through Edenred. Pricing is quote-based. Benefit platforms in general cost about 5–20 € per employee per month (vendor range), so a single add-on service has to fit well under that.

### Cited Findings
- Edenred defines Legal Care as "une assistance juridique proposée par une entreprise à ses collaborateurs" and partnered with Ekie (and Stairwage) in about 2023 to enrich its "care" offer. — [Edenred magazine](https://www.edenred.fr/magazine/notre-actualite/edenred-france/bien-etre-des-salaries-le-legal-care-nouveau-levier-qvt); [Edenred partners page](https://www.edenred.fr/nos-partenaires)
- Employee-benefit platforms typically cost 5–20 €/employee/month depending on the solution, headcount and service level. This is a vendor comparison (Klaro) and is not specific to legal services. — [Klaro, top 10 plateformes avantages salariés 2026](https://www.klaro.fr/blog/articles/top-10-avantages-salaries)
- Ekie's consumer price (9.90–19.90 €/month) is the only public price for an equivalent service. B2B is "on quote". — [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/professions/337-legaltech/80460-avostart-devient-ekie-service-legalcare-destination-entreprises.html); [ekie.pelostud.io](https://ekie.pelostud.io/)
- LADN discusses legaltech as an employee benefit ("legal care"), but no figures were captured. — [LADN business](https://business.ladn.eu/experts-metiers/management-rh/service-legaltech-avantage-salarial-entreprise-legalcare/)

### Inferences
- B2B per-head prices for legal care are typically a small fraction of the consumer price, because usage rates are low. That would give a likely band of about 1–4 €/employee/month. This is an inference, NOT sourced; confirm by asking Ekie or Edenred for a quote, or through buyers.
- Employers buy this as a wellbeing (QVT) or retention benefit, so the pitch should be about time saved for employees and fewer personal problems eating into work time, not "money recovered".

### Gaps
- No sourced prices for corporate concierge services (conciergerie d'entreprise) or for Ekie B2B per employee.
- No adoption rate data (share of employers offering legal care).

## 3. Mutuelles and insurers: legal protection/assistance in contracts, partnerships with legaltech, price per contract

### Takeaway
Legal protection (protection juridique) is a market of roughly 1.8–2 bn € in premiums, and about 32.9 million contracts include some legal-protection guarantee. The specialised insurers (Juridica/AXA, Covéa PJ, Solucia, CFDP) already run large in-house legal teams with amicable-resolution rates of 73–75%. They describe legaltechs as partners for digitalisation ("protection juridique augmentée"). No public figure was found for what an insurer pays a startup per contract.

### Cited Findings
- Market size: about 2 bn € in annual legal-protection premiums (Xerfi, year unclear). A comparator citing Xerfi gives 1.83 bn € collected in 2023, growing 5–6% a year. — [Xerfi](https://www.xerfi.com/presentationetude/le-marche-de-l-assurance-protection-juridique_abf20); [france-epargne.fr](https://www.france-epargne.fr/products/assurances-personnes/protection-juridique)
- 32.9 million insurance contracts of all types, including home multi-risk (MRH), included a legal-protection guarantee (2022, ministerial answer to a parliamentary question). — [Assemblée nationale QE n°2023](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q17/17-2023QE.htm)
- Covéa: 305 M€ of legal-protection premiums earned in 2024 (+4.9%). — [Covéa 2024 results (PDF)](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/communique_de_presse_resultats_covea_2024.pdf)
- Covéa Protection Juridique describes "protection juridique augmentée": insurance plus services plus partnerships with legaltechs, always seeking an amicable agreement first. — [L'Argus de l'assurance](https://www.argusdelassurance.com/assurance-de-personnes/covea-protection-juridique.139404)
- Juridica (AXA France subsidiary): 233 in-house lawyers, about 39,000 disputes a year, favourable outcome above 73%, solvency ratio 449.3% at end of 2024. Source is a directory, not Juridica's filings. — [Zevra, Juridica](https://zevra.tech/legaltechs/juridica/)
- CFDP: 75% of disputes settled amicably, 2,500 partner brokers (directory). — [Zevra annuaire](https://zevra.tech/en/annuaire-legaltechs)
- Solucia PJ: insurer specialised exclusively in legal protection. — [Zevra, Solucia](https://zevra.tech/legaltechs/solucia)
- A 2024 Les Echos Études report, as summarised by Village de la Justice: legaltechs compete with insurers on legal information but are "surtout des partenaires importants pour digitaliser et enrichir l'offre" and can make amicable resolution easier. — [Village de la Justice, État des lieux PJ 2025](https://www.village-justice.com/articles/etat-des-lieux-protection-juridique-2025,52297.html)
- An older interview (about 4 years old) with an insurer executive says "un certain nombre d'accords" had been concluded between legaltechs and legal-protection insurers. — [Village de la Justice](https://www.village-justice.com/articles/question-sur-image-des-protections,36993.html)
- Socrate Médiation (online mediation) is open only to policyholders of partner insurers, an example of a B2B2C model through insurers. — [Village de la Justice, MARD guide 2026](https://www.village-justice.com/articles/plateformes-resolution-amiable-des-differends-ligne,35629.html)
- Home insurance usually includes only "legal information" (a hotline). Full legal protection is an option or part of premium formulas. Legal assistance (information) is distinct from legal protection, which is an insurance contract under the Code des assurances. — [LegalPlace guide](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/assistance-juridique/); [MAIF](https://www.maif.fr/habitation/guide-assurance-habitation/garantie-aide-juridique)
- Complementary health contracts (mutuelles) can include medical and legal assistance guarantees. — [Les Pépites Tech](https://lespepitestech.com/blog/2025/12/15/mutuelle-pour-start-le-guide-pour-bien-choisir)
- A search summary attributed to the France Assureurs "L'assurance habitation en 2024" PDF a "prime moyenne de protection juridique de 471 € par contrat MRH". This is very likely a misreading: 471 € is implausible for legal protection alone and more likely an average claim cost or overall figure. DO NOT USE without checking. — [France Assureurs PDF](https://www.franceassureurs.fr/wp-content/uploads/lassurance-habitation-en-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- Implied average premium per legal-protection-bearing contract is about 55–60 €/year (≈1.8–2 bn € ÷ 32.9 M contracts). This is a rough inference that mixes standalone and embedded guarantees and different years. A service an insurer bolts on would therefore have to cost well under 1–3 €/contract/year to fit inside the premium. Inference, not sourced.
- Legal-protection insurers already do the "amicable resolution" step in-house with large teams. A startup is more likely to be bought as a digital self-service front-end (letters, LRAR automation, triage) that cuts handling cost per file, for example priced per file handled or as a platform licence. Pitching the service to members as a new benefit is the less likely sale.
- Assistance companies (IMA, Mondial/Allianz Partners, Europ Assistance) are the usual subcontractors behind mutuelle "assistance" guarantees. They are the likely buyers or integrators, not each mutuelle individually.

### Gaps
- No sourced price per contract per year paid for legal information/assistance services (Juridica, IMA, Europ Assistance, Mondial Assistance). These are confidential B2B contracts.
- No named 2023–2026 partnership between a mutuelle or legal-protection insurer and a consumer-dispute legaltech was found in the searches. The Argus or Les Echos archives would need a direct check.
- No official France Assureurs 2024 legal-protection premium figure was captured.

## 4. Neobanks/banks: legal assistance or dispute help for customers

### Takeaway
Banks mostly offer legal help through card insurance (overseas legal assistance, consumer-law help on card purchases) or paid add-on bundles (BoursoBank "Bourso Protect Plus": legal costs up to 15,000 € for identity theft and e-reputation). No evidence was found of Nickel, Revolut or Lydia offering a dispute-resolution service in France, or of a partnership model with a legaltech.

### Cited Findings
- BoursoBank includes overseas legal assistance with its Welcome, Ultim and Metal cards. Broader legal protection (legal costs up to 15,000 € for identity theft or e-reputation) is in the paid "Bourso Protect Plus" bundle. — [detective-banque.fr](https://www.detective-banque.fr/banque/boursorama-banque/protection-juridique-boursorama/)
- Card guarantees can include legal assistance for certain purchases, limited in practice to consumer law and with many exclusions. Often this is information only, with no cover for court costs. — [reassurez-moi.fr](https://reassurez-moi.fr/guide/protection-juridique/carte-bancaire)
- Revolut complaints go by email first, then to the UK ombudsman. For Nickel, the competent mediator is the ASF mediator (older article, to verify). — [pricebank.fr](https://www.pricebank.fr/questions-reponses/questions-frequentes/mediateur-bancaire.html)

### Inferences
- Banks are a channel where the product would be a paid add-on inside an insurance/assistance bundle, usually via the bank's insurer partner. That makes the route through an insurer or assistance company more realistic than selling directly to a neobank.

### Gaps
- No Nickel, Revolut or Lydia legal-assistance offers or partnership terms found. No pricing.

## 5. Sales cycle and procurement realities for a solo founder

### Takeaway
No hard sourced data on sales-cycle length was found. The evidence that does exist points to large intermediaries (Edenred, Up, Swile, Covéa, AXA/Juridica) that buy through formal partnerships, accelerator programmes and pilots (POC).

### Cited Findings
- About 150 French startups are active in insurance and risk (France Fintech / Bpifrance Le Hub 2024). Insurers partner through their licence, and accelerators exist: La French Assurtech in Niort (mutual insurers, since 2018) and the EuraTechnologies programme with Natixis Assurances covering AssurTech and LegalTech. — [Bpifrance Big Media](https://bigmedia.bpifrance.fr/nos-dossiers/assurtech-ces-startups-qui-reinventent-lassurance); [Planet Fintech](https://www.planet-fintech.com/Natixis-Assurances-soutient-les-startups-de-l-AssurTech_a1003.html)
- POC contracts: an unclear scope can lead to disputes. A Grenoble appeal court ruling (October 2023) penalised a client for failing to set the test's objectives and timeline. Advice: set explicit go/no-go criteria. — [assurance-software.com](https://www.assurance-software.com/ecosysteme-insurtech-comment-integrer-les-innovations-des-startups-dans-votre-vieux-si/) (summary; UNVERIFIED detail)
- The State's "France Legaltech" programme (DGE, 2025): 10 winners out of 57 applications, offering mentoring and access to a network of public and private partners. This is a possible credibility lever. — [entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/la-dge/actualites/france-legaltech-dix-entreprises-laureates-pour-structurer-la-filiere-francaise)
- Kalidea sells one-year subscriptions to CSE without tacit renewal, so a renewal must be re-sold every year. — [Officiel CSE](https://www.officielce.com/fournisseur/fonctionnement-du-cse/assistance-et-aide-aux-salaries/kalidea-ce-groupe-up)

### Inferences
- Likely requirements, from general knowledge and NOT sourced in this research:
  - a GDPR data-processing agreement, with data hosted in the EU
  - security questionnaires, sometimes an ISO 27001 or SOC 2 expectation
  - professional liability insurance (RC Pro)
  - for insurers, outsourcing/ICT third-party due diligence under DORA (in force since January 2025)
  - a clear line between legal information and regulated legal advice (the périmètre du droit under the 1971 law)
  
  Sales cycles of 6–18 months are commonly reported for insurers and large employers, but that is not sourced here.
- For a solo founder, the most feasible B2B2C path looks like this: become a partner listed on a CSE or benefits platform (Leeto, Swile, Kalidea, Edenred), which already handles procurement and distribution, then go after direct CSE deals in firms with 50+ employees. Insurers and mutuelles are a later, longer cycle.

### Gaps
- No sourced figures on sales-cycle length, pilot pricing, or minimum security and insurance requirements for CSE platforms, mutuelles or insurers.
- The "périmètre du droit" risk (whether drafting letters for third parties counts as regulated legal consultation) was not researched here.
